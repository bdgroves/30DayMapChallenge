A 3D map of California at dusk, looking north up the Central Valley. Gold arcs (people leaving Tuolumne County) and blue arcs (people arriving) rise from Sonora; the tallest reach Stanislaus, Calaveras, Sacramento and the Bay Area, while low, thin arcs run to Los Angeles, San Diego, Reno and Phoenix. A table lists the biggest flows, led by Stanislaus with 1,104 out and 1,877 in.
