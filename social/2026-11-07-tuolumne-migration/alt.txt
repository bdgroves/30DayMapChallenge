A 14-second orbit over a 3D map of California at dusk. Gold and blue arcs rise out of Sonora in Tuolumne County: gold for people moving out, blue for people moving in, over five years of IRS data. The tallest arcs reach Stanislaus, Calaveras and the Bay Area counties, while low, thin arcs run south toward Los Angeles and San Diego and east to Reno. County lines cross the land, and labels fade in: Stanislaus 1,104 out and 1,877 in.
