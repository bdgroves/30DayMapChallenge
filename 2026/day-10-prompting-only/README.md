## A map made only by asking

Big Creek's watershed above Groveland as a 1960s line-printer map: a FORTRAN program, written entirely from a one-sentence prompt, prints the basin in eight overprinted tones on green-bar paper, the way SYMAP did. The prompts are posted with it.

**Data**

- [USGS NLDI (basin and flowlines for gage 11284400)](https://labs.waterdata.usgs.gov/about-nldi/)
- [Copernicus DEM GLO-30](https://registry.opendata.aws/copernicus-dem/)

**Tools:** Claude, FORTRAN (gfortran), Python

**Prep:** Drafted. Rule: no hand edits; every change is asked for in words and logged in PROMPTS.md.

**Go deeper**

- [The prompts, and the FORTRAN](https://github.com/bdgroves/30DayMapChallenge/tree/main/2026/day-10-prompting-only)

Folder: `data/` for downloads (not committed), `out/` for the finished map (`map.png`, `alt.txt`).
