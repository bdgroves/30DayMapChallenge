Map of the Pacific Northwest showing how strongly people felt the magnitude 6.8 Nisqually earthquake of February 28, 2001. Small squares coloured from pale (weak) to dark red (very strong) cover western Washington, strongest around the epicenter at the south end of Puget Sound near Olympia, Lakewood and Tacoma, and fading toward Portland, Vancouver and Spokane. Based on 10,725 'Did You Feel It?' reports.
