Map of 1,190 geocaches found by Hipparchus from 2001 to 2026, as small dots coloured from pale gold for early years to dark red for recent ones. The main map covers the West Coast, where most are, thickest in Washington, California, Nevada. A small world map below shows finds in 25 countries, and a bar chart shows finds per year, with the most in 2017. The farthest finds: north in Iceland, south in South Island, NZ, east in North Island, NZ, west in Hawaii, and farthest from home in Tasmania, Australia, 13,421 km away.
