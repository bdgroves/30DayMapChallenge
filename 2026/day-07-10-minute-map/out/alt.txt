Dark map of the Los Angeles basin and its mountains, covered in 2,438 overlapping fire perimeters since 1950, colored from dark brown for the oldest to pale tan for the newest; burned ground blankets the Santa Monica, San Gabriel and Santa Susana mountains. The Palisades and Eaton fires of January 2025 are outlined in white, on the coast west of Santa Monica and above Altadena. A clock in the corner reads 05:09 of 10:00.
