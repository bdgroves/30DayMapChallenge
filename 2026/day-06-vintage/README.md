## Tasman to Cook

New Zealand's coast as Abel Tasman charted it in 1642 and James Cook in 1769–70, over the modern coastline, engraved in an 18th-century style. A map version of my Cartographic Perspectives article.

**Data**

- [David Rumsey Map Collection (Tasman and Cook charts, georeferenced)](https://www.davidrumsey.com/)
- [Natural Earth coastline](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/)

**Tools:** QGIS georeferencer, Python

**Prep:** Find and georeference the two charts; the slowest prep of the month.

Folder: `data/` for downloads (not committed), `out/` for the finished map (`map.png`, `alt.txt`).
