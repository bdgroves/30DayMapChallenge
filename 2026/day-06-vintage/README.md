## Tasman to Cook

New Zealand's coast as Abel Tasman charted it in 1642 and James Cook in 1769–70, over the modern coastline, engraved in an 18th-century style. A map version of my Cartographic Perspectives article.

**Data**

- [Cook's Chart of New-Zealand (1772), Royal Museums Greenwich F0293, via Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Chart_of_New_Zealand,_explored_in_1769_and_1770_by_Lieut._I-_Cook,Commander_of_His_Majesty%27s_Bark_Endeavour._RMG_F0293.tiff)
- [Tasman map (c. 1644), State Library of New South Wales, via Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tasman_Map_SLNSWSLNSW_FL19288478.jpg)
- [Natural Earth coastline](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/)

**Tools:** Python, geopandas, matplotlib

**Prep:** Drafted: Cook's chart georeferenced from its own Mercator graticule (fit within 3 px), today's coast over it, Tasman's coast and an inset of his chart.

Folder: `data/` for downloads (not committed), `out/` for the finished map (`map.png`, `alt.txt`).
