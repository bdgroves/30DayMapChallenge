**Thursday, November 5, 2026** · status: ✏️ draft

## Where you can see Rainier from

A viewshed from the summit of Mount Rainier over 30 m terrain, with Earth's curvature and refraction, showing every place in Washington with a line of sight to the mountain.

**Data**

- [Copernicus DEM GLO-30 (AWS open data)](https://registry.opendata.aws/copernicus-dem/)

**Tools:** Python, rasterio, a viewshed routine (GDAL gdal_viewshed)

**Prep:** Drafted (Copernicus GLO-90 and gdal_viewshed).

Folder: `data/` for downloads (not committed), `out/` for the finished map (`map.png`, `alt.txt`).
